# Style 10: Dark Ember Editorial & Macro UI Focus (Claude Dark Promo Inspired)

Use this direction for flagship dark-mode AI launches, developer/creative tools, and prestige brand teasers combining warm editorial serif typography with deep macro peeks inside real dark UI.

## Visual Signature & Surface Physics

1. **Obsidian Canvas + Breathing Ember Waves:**
   - Pure obsidian/midnight ground (`#050506`) lit from below by slow-drifting crimson-to-terracotta glow waves (`#8F120A -> #E63917 -> #FF6B2B`) that breathe continuously (`ambientBreath` + `pulseGlow`) behind typography and UI.
2. **Warm Luminous Serif + Precision Dark UI:**
   - Pair refined editorial serif display headlines (`Instrument Serif`, `Playfair Display`, or `Newsreader`) carrying subtle warm amber/white outer bloom (`text-shadow`) with razor-sharp sans-serif dark-mode UI (`#141416`, `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`).
3. **Sparking Focus Accents:**
   - Short warm light arcs frame the opening brand mark and prompt entry without cluttering reading zones.

## Signature Choreography Moves

1. **Sweeping Light-Arc Opener:**
   - Curved crimson light ring sweeps around the centered mark (`0–2.0s`), settling cleanly into a glass prompt bar rising from below.
2. **Deep Macro Dropdown / Control Switch:**
   - Instead of showing the entire app at `1.0x`, zoom deeply (`2.0x–2.4x`) directly onto an interactive dropdown/model picker (`Sonnet 4.6 -> Opus 4.7`), click to open, hover-highlight row, and click to commit with `buttonPressScale` + subtle `cameraRecoil`.
3. **Pull-Back Stream + 3-Beat Anchor Word Swap:**
   - Pull back cleanly while luminous response lines fade up, then finish on a crisp 3-beat finale where the brand word stays locked on the left and the glowing action verb swaps rapidly (`Brand Code -> Brand Build -> Brand Learn`).
