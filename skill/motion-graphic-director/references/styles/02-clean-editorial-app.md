# Style 02: Clean Editorial App (Gojek-Inspired Calm Clarity)

Use this direction for everyday utility apps, onboarding walkthroughs, education tools, and trust-first explainers where readability and calm spatial rhythm beat heavy visual effects.

## Visual Signature & Surface Physics

1. **Daylight Minimal Ground:**
   - Warm ivory, alabaster, or crisp daylight off-white (`#F8F9FC` / `#F7F6F2` / `#FFFFFF`) with huge clean negative space (`35%–45%` open canvas).
   - No dark vignettes, no neon glow halos, no sci-fi grid patterns.
2. **Airy Elevated Surface System (`max 2` focal planes):**
   - Pure matte card containers (`#FFFFFF`) separated by subtle hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) and wide diffuse shadows (`0 20px 50px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`).
   - High-contrast ink-black headlines (`#0F172A`), neutral slate descriptions (`#475569`), and **1 disciplined brand accent color** (e.g., crisp emerald `#00880C`, cobalt `#1D4ED8`, or warm terracotta `#E04F33`) used only for active states, selection badges, and primary CTAs.
3. **Typography:**
   - Clean humanist or geometric grotesque (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, or `DM Sans`) with tight display tracking (`-0.03em`) and concise scannable micro-copy (`<= 8 words` per focal point).

## Pacing, Camera & Audio Fit

- **Reference Benchmark (Gojek `14.3s` promo):** **`~80%` calm breathing time** vs `~20%` crisp snappy motion!
- Focus on **one clear core interaction per 4–5 seconds** instead of rushing multiple UI modules.
- Camera technique: subtle push-in (`1.00 -> 1.06`) while holding focus cleanly, crisp curved pointer tap with elastic card lift (`scale 1.03`), and clean `rackFocusMetrics` blur (`blurPx` 6–10px) pushing inactive cards gently backward while the active item glides forward.
- Audio match: crisp clean acoustic clicks, soft paper/card slide, subtle bright marimba or Rhodes chord chime, generous silence between UI beats (`dyn_p95_p10_db >= 15 dB`).
