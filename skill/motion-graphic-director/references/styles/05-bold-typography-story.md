# Style 05: Bold Typography Story (Rhythm & Statement Driven)

Use this direction for thought-leadership reels, product launch manifestos, founder story hooks, before/after contrast reels, and rapid problem-agitation openers where typography itself carries the visual drama.

## Visual Signature & Layout Discipline

1. **Scale & Weight Contrast Without Clutter:**
   - Pair oversized ExtraBold statements (`80–116px`) with crisp Light phrasing (`300`) or quiet understated monospaced metadata (`22–24px`).
   - Strict word economy: max **`4–7` words per beat** cleanly aligned inside `X = 84..996px`, `Y = 255..1640px`. Never center dense 4-line paragraphs.
2. **Tactile Typographic Devices:**
   - **Strike-through Critique:** Hold quoted misconception clearly (`1.8s+` calm read), then sweep a crisp pill underline/strikethrough (`scaleX`) while tilting/cooling the text (`-2deg`) before snapping to truth.
   - **Wipe-Reveal Stamp:** Sweep a solid white or accent rounded box across the hero word (`transformOrigin: "left center"`, `scaleX`), inverting text color mid-wipe.
   - **Contrast Flip:** Instant scale-out portal from dark ground -> high-contrast solid color punch -> calm dark/glass proof.

## Pacing, Camera & Audio Fit

- **Silence Is an Instrument:** Place a **`230–240 ms` Silent Drop** (`bgm_vol = 0.02`) immediately prior to the hero statement stamp, followed by warm sub-thump (`lowpass=f=240`) + soft dry pop on frame impact.
- Pair micro camera recoil (`0.035` zoom dip + `10px` Y kick decaying over `14 frames`) with keyframe `rotX`/`rotZ` tilt changes (`1.5–2.5deg` max) so text feels weighted and physical rather than sliding flat across a screen.
- Avoid harsh gunshot, glass-shatter, or metal-clang stock sounds; favor deep velvet sub drops and crisp ASMR percussive pops.
