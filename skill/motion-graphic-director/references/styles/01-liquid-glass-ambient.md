# Style 01: Liquid Glass + Dark Ambient Silk

Use this direction for modern flagship AI products, sleek SaaS demos, and dark crystal UI showcases where translucent optical surfaces must sit over luminous sculptural backdrops without turning muddy or low-contrast.

## Visual Signature & Surface Physics

1. **Sculptural Ambient Ground:**
   - Deep midnight base (`#04062A` / `#070B44`) intersected by smooth 3D draped silk folds shifting through electric royal cobalt (`#1438E6` / `#245BFF`) to velvet periwinkle-lavender highlights (`#9AA4F8` / `#DDD9FF`).
   - Place darker sapphire troughs directly under open typography so headlines naturally achieve high contrast, while grazing glass rims with brighter silk crests.
2. **True Optical Liquid Glass (`max 2` glass elements per screen):**
   - Crystal interior: low-to-medium optical tint (`rgba(16, 36, 130, 0.42)` to `rgba(4, 8, 46, 0.64)`) + `backdropFilter: blur(16px) saturate(165%) brightness(1.08)`. Never cover the entire screen in frosted white milk.
   - Directional Fresnel rim: conic/linear specular border highlight (`rgba(255,255,255,0.86)` at top-left catching light -> soft indigo taper -> secondary periwinkle rim reflection).
   - Outer depth shadow (`0 28px 66px rgba(2, 5, 32, 0.62)`) + subtle inner top catchlight meniscus.
3. **Typography & Contrast Discipline:**
   - Expressive sans weight pairing within the same lockup: **ExtraBold (`800`) paired with Light (`300`)** in pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with cool indigo-white secondary copy (`#E2E8FF` / `#D6DEFF`).
   - Reserve monospace (`IBM Plex Mono`) strictly for compact codes (`M0`–`M5`) or repository URLs — never uppercase tracked eyebrow pills.
   - Use 1 factual soft tint max (e.g., luminous coral `#FFC7BF` for errors/warnings) — **zero glowing traffic-light dots**.
   - Target `>= 4.5:1` WCAG AA contrast on all body/label pixels and `>= 12:1` for primary headlines.

## Camera, Motion & Lighting Fit

- Smooth floating backdrop drift (`±14–18px` sine motion), clamped parallax (`<= ±34px`) during macro camera zooms so backdrop edges stay hidden.
- Tactile cursor interactions boost the glass rim reflection (`hoverBoost`) on approach before physical recoil (`buttonPressScale`).
- Works exceptionally well paired with velvety dark audio aesthetics (Warm Jazz Lo-Fi, Rhodes electric piano, and soft ASMR liquid/bubble pops + creamy Topre keystrokes).

## Reference Pairings Across 6 Acts

- Use dark Liquid Glass for interactive prompt entry, 3-slab depth rack-focus, 4-tab horizontal crystal dock, and serene outro card.
- Optional contrast break (Act 2 dramatic turn): briefly morph the ambient stage to deep velvet crimson (`#7F153B -> #120416`) for a 3-beat typographic reality check before diving back into cobalt 3D glass.
